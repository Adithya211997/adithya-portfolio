P-03 — Education

IUIS Invento — UZEB International School

← Prev — MSR City All Work Next — Burnaby Road →

BIM management and multidisciplinary modelling for an international school campus in Uzbekistan.

Alongside King’s School Abu Dhabi, IUIS Invento — the UZEB International School — is one of two international education campuses I oversee BIM management and modelling for at Sculpt Space.

My role centres on ensuring accurate multidisciplinary alignment and documentation: coordinating the architectural model against structural and MEP submissions, maintaining model standards, and keeping the federated model clash-free as the design develops.
