A future-focused K–12 international campus on Fahid Island — the regional extension of King’s College School Wimbledon.

Project Overview

King’s College School Abu Dhabi is a major international education project developed as the regional extension of the renowned King’s College School Wimbledon. Located on Fahid Island, Abu Dhabi, the campus is planned as a future-focused K–12 educational environment integrating learning, sports, wellbeing and community-focused spaces.

The project is designed to accommodate up to 2,220 students across a 50,000 m² campus, with the wider vision placing education within a natural and wellbeing-oriented setting. The campus is expected to open in 2028, subject to the required approvals.

My Involvement

I am contributing to the project as an Architect and BIM Coordinator at Sculpt Space Pvt. Ltd., working in association with DARC Dubai Architects.

My role combines architectural design development, technical coordination, BIM management and multidisciplinary model coordination. I work across the interface between design intent, architectural documentation and digital project delivery, ensuring that architectural information is accurately developed, coordinated and maintained throughout the BIM workflow.

Architectural Responsibilities

As the project Architect, my responsibilities involve translating architectural design intent into coordinated and technically developed project information.

Design Development

  • Develop architectural design information in accordance with the project’s design intent and requirements.
  • Develop and maintain detailed architectural models using Autodesk Revit.
  • Translate design concepts and architectural information into coordinated BIM models.
  • Develop plans, elevations, sections, details and other architectural documentation.
  • Review architectural layouts and ensure consistency between drawings and the BIM model.
  • Assist with design development and refinement of architectural elements.
  • Coordinate architectural requirements with structural, MEP and other specialist disciplines.
  • Review design changes and incorporate approved revisions into the architectural model.
  • Maintain consistency between design information, model geometry and project documentation.

Technical Architecture

  • Develop architectural elements to the required level of detail and information.
  • Review constructability and coordination requirements during model development.
  • Produce and coordinate technical drawings and documentation.
  • Review interfaces between architectural elements and other disciplines.
  • Identify design conflicts and technical coordination requirements before documentation and delivery.
  • Support the resolution of architectural and multidisciplinary design issues.
  • Maintain drawing and model accuracy through continuous design development and review.
BIM Coordination Responsibilities

As the BIM Coordinator, I am responsible for maintaining a structured BIM workflow and supporting multidisciplinary coordination across the project.

BIM Model Management

  • Manage and coordinate the architectural BIM model within the project environment.
  • Establish and maintain model organisation, worksharing and model development workflows.
  • Monitor model quality, accuracy and consistency.
  • Maintain appropriate model structure, naming conventions and information standards.
  • Review model development against project BIM requirements.
  • Support the implementation of BIM standards and project-specific modelling procedures.
  • Monitor model performance and identify opportunities for optimisation.

Multidisciplinary Coordination

  • Coordinate the architectural model with Structural, MEP and other consultant disciplines.
  • Develop and review federated models for multidisciplinary coordination.
