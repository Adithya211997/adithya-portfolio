P-10 — Residential — Weekend Retreat

Farm House, Kunigal

← Prev — Mr. Venkatesh Residence All Work Next — Verderecreo: Urban Lagoon →

A pool-side weekend retreat blending courtyard living with a timber deck, pergola and native landscaping.

Designed as a weekend escape outside Bengaluru, this farmhouse pairs an outdoor living courtyard with a private pool, timber deck and pergola, wrapped in native planting. A second, more contemporary massing option explored a sharper material palette of dark cladding and timber screening for the same brief.

My contribution covered the resort-style planning of bedrooms, kitchen and living spaces around the pool axis, and coordination of the exterior renders used to compare massing and material options with the client.
