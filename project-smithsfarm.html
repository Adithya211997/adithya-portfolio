P-01 — Residential — Affordable Housing

Smith’s Farm

← Prev — Integrated Sports Hub All Work Next — MSR City →

A 220-home affordable housing scheme wrapped around a landscaped public square in Ealing.

Smith’s Farm replaces a lorry park and disused commercial yard on Kensington Road with 220 new affordable homes, all offered at London Affordable Rent or shared ownership — 60% at the borough’s neediest tenure band. Seven blocks (A through G) frame a central landscaped public park, with the massing stepped down toward existing residential neighbours to protect daylight and privacy.

As Architectural Assistant and BIM Architect on the project, I worked across RIBA Stages 3–5: producing coordinated construction packages, technical wall/floor/roof build-up details, and M4(2) accessible bathroom layouts, while integrating the architectural model with structural and MEP teams in Revit and Navisworks. Clash detection workflows I helped refine cut resolution time by roughly 30%, and tighter BIM documentation reduced drawing errors by around 25%.
