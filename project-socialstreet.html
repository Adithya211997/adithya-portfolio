P-08 — Mixed-Use — Retail & F&B

Social Street

← Prev — Mr. Mune Gowda Villa All Work Next — Mr. Venkatesh Residence →

A co-working and multi-cuisine food-street development along Electronic City, stepping retail terraces down to a landscaped pedestrian spine.

Social Street reimagines a stretch of Electronic City as a pedestrian-first social spine, combining ground-floor food and retail units with co-working space above. The section steps back at each level to create shaded terraces and seating overlooking the street.

My work on the scheme covered the ground and first-floor plans and the site section, coordinating the retail frontage, circulation cores and landscaped terraces that carry the development’s social-street concept through to the public realm.
