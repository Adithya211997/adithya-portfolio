P-05 — Residential — Apartments

Lorne Park Road

← Prev — Burnaby Road All Work Next — Grano Interiors & Fit-Out →

Demolition of a semi-detached dwelling and erection of a 4-storey, 10-flat building in Bournemouth.

Lorne Park Road involved the demolition of a large semi-detached dwelling and its replacement with a 4-storey, 10-flat building. The design was completed with the ARC planning team and passed to me to complete the initial preliminary drawing pack.

The project raised several coordination challenges: a sub-terranean cycle store required specification input from the ground-works team, new fire regulations meant cladding and roof build-ups had to be specified carefully against manufacturer recommendations, and clear detailing was needed to meet visual privacy requirements for neighbouring properties.
