P-09 — Residential — Private Villa

Mr. Venkatesh Residence

← Prev — Social Street All Work Next — Farm House, Kunigal →

A classically detailed family residence of 2,535 sq ft at ground level and 2,622 sq ft above, organised around a central family living space.

The Venkatesh Residence is a two-storey classical villa built around a central family living space that ties together dedicated puja, guest and parents’ suites on the ground floor, with a family living room and bedroom suites above.

Arched openings, a pitched tiled roof and a symmetrical street elevation give the house a formal street presence, while the internal plan keeps everyday family life centred on the double-height family room.
