P-12 — University Thesis — Stadium & Sports City

Integrated Sports Hub

← Prev — Verderecreo: Urban Lagoon All Work Next — Smith’s Farm →

A 72-acre multi-sport training city built around a retractable-roof circular stadium, designed to help India build a serious bid for a mega sporting event.

India has never hosted an Olympic Games, and the country’s sporting infrastructure remains concentrated around a handful of disciplines. Integrated Sports Hub proposes a 72-acre training city on the outskirts of Bengaluru, capable of hosting cricket, football and athletics under one adaptable roof — sharing infrastructure across sports rather than building a stadium for each.

At its centre sits a circular, retractable-tiered stadium with a star-shaped operable roof, surrounded by an aquatic centre, indoor arena, open-air theatre and dedicated training pitches. My thesis explored the stadium’s bowl geometry and roof-truss logic, developed the 1:800 site plan and detailed sections, and built a physical study model to test the retractable roof concept.
