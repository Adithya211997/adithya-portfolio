P-07 — Residential — Private Villa

Mr. Mune Gowda Villa

← Prev — Grano Interiors & Fit-Out All Work Next — Social Street →

A multi-level private residence organised around a central courtyard, with a home theatre, entertainment deck and rooftop terrace garden.

This four-storey private villa is organised around a central courtyard that threads light and ventilation through every level — from the formal living and dining rooms on the ground floor, through family and guest suites above, to a home theatre, office and terrace garden on the top floor.

As Junior Architect on the project I worked on the working drawing set for the ground and first floors, developing plans, courtyard detailing and the sequence of formal versus family living spaces, alongside the third-floor terrace and home-theatre layout.
