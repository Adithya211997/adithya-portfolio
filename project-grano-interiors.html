P-06 — Interiors — Residential & Commercial

Grano Interiors & Fit-Out

← Prev — Lorne Park Road All Work Next — Mr. Mune Gowda Villa →

Kitchen and interior coordination across Grano’s UK residential and commercial projects — from designer plan to final built result.

Alongside apartment-block delivery, I supported Grano Design Works’ interiors work: taking a bespoke kitchen from designer plan through 3D render to the completed installation, and producing visualisation for flat interiors, a ground-floor food court, and an office lobby fit-out on adjoining commercial schemes.

This work sat alongside my technical documentation role, giving clients a clear before/after picture of how the specified joinery and finishes would read once built.
