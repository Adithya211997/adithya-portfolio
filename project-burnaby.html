Burnaby Road

A 12-flat beachfront accommodation on the Bournemouth coast, taken from planning approval to a full preliminary drawing pack.

Burnaby Road is a beachfront property on the Bournemouth coast. Working with the ARC planning team, the scheme was designed as 12 flats; once planning was approved, the design passed to the building technology stage, where I was assigned to produce the full preliminary drawing pack and accompanying schedules.

I was responsible for producing the majority of the technical design — wall, floor and roof build-ups, opening and foundation details — and recommended several changes to ground-floor party wall locations and specifications to ensure a consistent cavity and optimum vertical load distribution.
