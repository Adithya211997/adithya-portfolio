P-02 — Masterplan — Mixed-Use Residential

MSR City

← Prev — Smith’s Farm All Work Next — IUIS Invento — UZEB International School →

A 62-acre mixed-use development of 3,200+ residential units, commercial zones and clubhouse facilities.

MSR City is a large-scale township on the outskirts of Bengaluru, developed for Godrej with Architect Hafeez Contractor and Design Cell. The 62-acre masterplan combines 3,200+ residential units across multiple clusters with commercial frontage and shared clubhouse amenities.

I coordinate architectural design and BIM delivery for the site, running multidisciplinary coordination with Buro Happold, Architect Hafeez Contractor and Design Cell to keep federated models clash-free and consultants aligned. This includes leading architectural design packages and detailed coordination drawings for the residential clusters, implementing BIM standards and model audits across teams, and issuing weekly Navisworks clash reports to streamline decisions.
